WML Bio Tech is an agricultural biotechnology company dedicated to empowering farmers with **high‑quality, scientifically developed seeds**. Our mission is to maximize crop productivity while promoting sustainable farming practices.

---

## 🚀 Vision
To revolutionize agriculture by providing farmers with innovative seed solutions that ensure **better yields, soil health, and long‑term sustainability**.

## 🌍 Mission
- Deliver premium seeds tested for **germination, purity, and performance**.  
- Invest in **research and innovation** to develop resilient crop varieties.  
- Support **environmentally responsible farming** for a greener future.  
- Put **farmers first** with on‑field guidance and agronomy support.

## ✨ Features
- 🌱 **Premium Seed Quality** – Rigorous testing for reliability.  
- 🔬 **Research & Innovation** – Continuous development of improved varieties.  
- 🌾 **Higher Crop Yield** – Designed to maximize harvests.  
- 🌍 **Sustainable Agriculture** – Eco‑friendly practices for long‑term growth.  
- 🤝 **Farmer Support** – Guidance and post‑sale assistance.
